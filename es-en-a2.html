Section 0How to use this course

Level A2: you learn to talk about the past, make plans, ask politely, and use object pronouns that were only hinted at in A1. Pronunciation, ser / estar / tener, gender and numbers are covered in the A1 course.

By the end you will be able to

  • talk about the past with the preterite (hablé, comí) and the imperfect (hablaba, vivía);
  • talk about the future (voy a trabajar, trabajaré);
  • ask politely (quisiera, ¿podría…?);
  • use object pronouns (lo veo, le doy, me gusta);
  • compare, build complex sentences and write a letter.
Do not memorise tables first. Learn each tense through the question it answers and typical phrases: Ayer comí con mi hermano. (Yesterday I ate with my brother.) Mañana voy a trabajar.
False friends. largo = long (not large: grande), ropa = clothes, sopa = soup, fábrica = factory, carpeta = folder, molestar = to bother, soportar = to bear, introducir = to insert, campo = countryside, nudo = knot.

Section 2The future

Spanish has two ways: the everyday ir a + infinitive and the simple future, which adds endings to the whole infinitive.

Section 4Object pronouns

Spanish puts short pronouns before the conjugated verb: Lo veo. Te quiero. Me gusta.

Rules

1. With an infinitive the pronoun can attach to the end: Quiero verlo = Lo quiero ver. 2. With two pronouns, indirect comes first: Me lo da. 3. Le/les + lo/la becomes se lo/la: Se lo doy. (I give it to him.)

Section 12Reading: three texts

Text 1. Mi familia

Me llamo Ana y vivo en Madrid. Tengo marido y dos hijos. Mi marido es médico y yo soy profesora. Nuestro hijo tiene diez años y nuestra hija siete. Los fines de semana vamos a menudo a la montaña. En verano queremos ir a la playa.

My name is Ana and I live in Madrid. I have a husband and two children. My husband is a doctor and I am a teacher. Our son is ten and our daughter seven. At weekends we often go to the mountains. In summer we want to go to the beach.

Text 2. Ayer

Ayer me levanté a las siete, desayuné y fui al trabajo en autobús. Estuve en la oficina hasta las cinco. Luego compré pan y leche. Por la noche preparé la cena y vi una película. A las once me acosté.

Yesterday I got up at seven, had breakfast and went to work by bus. I was in the office until five. Then I bought bread and milk. In the evening I made dinner and watched a film. At eleven I went to bed.

Text 3. Planes para el verano

Este verano iré a la playa con unos amigos. Alquilaremos una casa cerca del mar. Nadaremos, jugaremos al voleibol y por la noche cenaremos en la terraza. Si hace buen tiempo, nos quedaremos dos semanas.

This summer I'll go to the beach with some friends. We'll rent a house near the sea. We'll swim, play volleyball and have dinner on the terrace at night. If the weather is good, we'll stay two weeks.
