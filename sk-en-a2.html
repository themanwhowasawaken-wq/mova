Section 0How to use this course

Level A2: you learn to talk about the past, make plans, ask politely, and use the cases that were only hinted at in A1. Pronunciation, byť, mať, gender and numbers are covered in the A1 course.

By the end you will be able to

  • talk about the past (robil som, bola som) and the future (budem, napíšem);
  • understand the idea of verb aspect (imperfective and perfective);
  • ask politely and express wishes (chcel by som, mohli by ste);
  • use the genitive, dative, locative and instrumental in everyday phrases;
  • compare, build complex sentences and write a letter.
Do not memorise tables first. Learn each case through the question it answers and the typical phrases: do školy (to school), bez cukru (without sugar), s bratom (with my brother), v Bratislave (in Bratislava).
False friends. pozor = attention, škoda = pity, mŕtvy = dead, dokonca = even, Vianoce = Christmas, zámok = castle and lock, kosť = bone, pokoj = peace.

Section 1The past tense

The past is made with the l-participle and a form of byť: robil som (I did). The participle agrees with the subject in gender and number.

How to form it

Take the infinitive, drop -ť and add -l: robiť → robil, hovoriť → hovoril, byť → bol, mať → mal. In the third person there is no som/si. In the plural, masculine and feminine are the same (robili) in Slovak, unlike Czech. Irregular: ísť → išiel / išla, povedať → povedal, môcť → mohol, jesť → jedol, piť → pil, čítať → čítal.

Section 2The future and verb aspect

Almost every Slovak verb has two aspects: imperfective (a process or habit) and perfective (a result, a completed act). They often look like different words: písať / napísať.

Key rule

A perfective verb has no real present: its "present" forms refer to the future. Napíšem ti zajtra. = I will write to you tomorrow (and finish it). Budem ti písať. = I will be writing to you (process).

Section 13Reading: three texts

Text 1. Moja rodina

Volám sa Anna a bývam v Bratislave. Mám manžela a dve deti. Manžel je lekár a ja som učiteľka. Náš syn má desať rokov a dcéra sedem. Cez víkend často chodíme do hôr. V lete chceme ísť k moru.

My name is Anna and I live in Bratislava. I have a husband and two children. My husband is a doctor and I am a teacher. Our son is ten and our daughter seven. At weekends we often go to the mountains. In summer we want to go to the sea.

Text 2. Včerajšok

Včera som vstal o siedmej, najedol som sa a išiel som do práce autobusom. V kancelárii som bol do piatej. Potom som kúpil chlieb a mlieko. Večer som uvaril večeru a pozeral som film. O jedenástej som išiel spať.

Yesterday I got up at seven, had breakfast and went to work by bus. I was in the office until five. Then I bought bread and milk. In the evening I cooked dinner and watched a film. At eleven I went to bed.

Text 3. Plány na leto

Tento rok v lete pôjdem s kamarátmi k moru. Prenajmeme si domček pri pláži. Budeme plávať, hrať volejbal a večer sedieť pri ohni. Ak bude pekne, ostaneme dva týždne.

Example. A letter to a friend

Ďakujem za tvoj list. U nás je všetko v poriadku. Minulý týždeň som bol v Karpatoch. Bolo krásne počasie. Chcel by som sa tam vrátiť. A ako sa máš ty? Napíš mi čoskoro.
Maj sa pekne,
Michael
