Section 0How to use this course

Romanian is a Romance language surrounded by Slavic neighbours, so it feels like a mix of familiar and surprising. Many words are recognisable from Spanish, French or Italian, the spelling is close to the sound, and the grammar is friendlier than it looks. This course takes you to A1: introductions, family, food, directions, shopping, time and everyday matters.

What is on your side

  • Spelling matches sound. Once you know ă, â/î, ș, ț and the c / g rules, you can read anything aloud.
  • Latin vocabulary: familie, hotel, important, restaurant, telefon, student, carte, lună, pâine.
  • Friendly verbs. Regular verbs follow a few patterns, and "I am / I have" are easy: sunt, am.
  • No complicated case system at A1. Romanian has only a few cases, and most of the work is done by prepositions.
What will be new. The definite article is stuck on the end of the noun (casă → casa = the house), nouns have three genders, and the verb endings show the person, so subject pronouns are usually dropped.
Main tip. Learn phrases, not isolated words: not "a bea = to drink", but "beau apă = I drink water". Endings then come by themselves.
False friends. a pretinde = to claim (not pretend), librărie = bookshop (not library: bibliotecă), actual = current, eventual = possibly, primar = mayor, magazin = shop (and warehouse), sensibil = noticeable, a poseda = to own, pensie = pension and guest house, elev = school pupil.

How to work

  1. Read the explanations and tap words to hear them.
  2. Do the exercises here and on the Exercises page.
  3. 20–30 minutes a day. The plan is in section 12.
About the pronunciation column. It is a simple English-style respelling. It is approximate, so rely on the audio. "uh" is a short vowel like the one in about; "ih" is the Romanian â / î (a deeper, more closed sound, a bit like the "i" in roses). Stress varies from word to word and is not marked.

Diphthongs

Romanian has many: ea ("ya"), oa ("wa"), ia ("ya"), ie ("ye"), ai, ei, ou. For example seară [SYA-ruh], școală [shkwah-luh], fiecare.

Stress. There is no single rule: it is usually on the second-to-last or last syllable, and you learn it with the word. The endings often help: -ește, -ire stress the end. The audio will show you.

Section 3Nouns, gender and articles

Romanian has three genders: masculine m (blue), feminine f (red) and neuter n (green). The neuter is masculine in the singular and feminine in the plural.

Definite article

It is added to the end: feminine -ă → -a (casă → casa), -e → -ea (carte → cartea); masculine + -ul (copil → copilul) or + -le after -e (frate → fratele).

Negation

Put nu before the verb: Nu sunt. Nu am. Double negatives are normal: Nu am nimic. (I have nothing.) Nu vine nimeni.

Modal + să + verb

Romanian has little use for the infinitive after modals. It uses să + a conjugated verb: Pot să intru? Vreau să beau o cafea. Trebuie să lucrez. A trebui is impersonal; the person goes in the să verb. For a learned skill use a ști: Știu să înot.

Pot să intru? | poht suh eentroo | May I come in? Vreau să beau o cafea. | vryaoo suh byaoo oh kahfya | I want to drink a coffee. Azi trebuie să lucrez. | ahzee trehbooye suh lookrehz | I have to work today. Acum nu pot să vorbesc. | ahkoom noo poht suh vohrbehsk | I can't talk now. Știi să gătești? | shteeee suh guhtehshtee | Can you cook? Aici nu se fumează. | eyechee noo seh foomyazuh | Smoking is not allowed here. Aș dori un ceai, vă rog. | ahsh dohree oon cheye vuh rohg | I would like a tea, please.

Section 11Four more dialogues

# 1. La medic (at the doctor's) Medic: Bună ziua, ce probleme aveți? | Good day, what's troubling you? Pacient: Mă doare capul și am febră. | I have a headache and a fever. Medic: De când? | Since when? Pacient: De două zile. | For two days. Medic: Luați aceste pastile și rămâneți acasă. | Take these tablets and stay at home. Pacient: Mulțumesc, domnule doctor. | Thank you, doctor. # 2. La magazinul de haine (in a clothes shop) Vânzătoare: Vă pot ajuta? | Can I help you? Client: Caut o jachetă. | I'm looking for a jacket. Vânzătoare: Ce mărime purtați? | What size do you wear? Client: Mărimea M. Pot s-o probez? | Size M. Can I try it on? Vânzătoare: Desigur, cabinele sunt acolo. | Of course, the fitting rooms are over there. Client: Cât costă? | How much is it? Vânzătoare: Două sute de lei. | Two hundred lei. # 3. La hotel (at the hotel) Oaspete: Bună seara, am o rezervare. | Good evening, I have a reservation. Recepționer: Pe ce nume? | In what name? Oaspete: Smith. O cameră single pentru trei nopți. | Smith. A single room for three nights. Recepționer: Poftiți cheia. Micul dejun este de la șapte la zece. | Here is your key. Breakfast is from seven to ten. Oaspete: Există wi-fi? | Is there Wi-Fi? Recepționer: Da, parola este pe cheie. | Yes, the password is on the key. # 4. La telefon (on the phone) Anna: Alo? | Hello? Marcu: Salut Anna, sunt Marcu. Ce mai faci? | Hi Anna, it's Marcu.
